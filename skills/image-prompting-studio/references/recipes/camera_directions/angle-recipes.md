# Twelve-shot camera recipes

Load this bank for a curated shoot or a specific recipe. The original detailed templates and examples below are preserved as craft. Twelve is this preset's count, not a studio default. Where a recipe introduces flash, sunset, gesture, color treatment, 16:9 or 4K, use it only if compatible with the requested shot. For camera-only work, preserve the reference and adapt these example choices. Descriptions such as cinematic quality or emotional effect are creative intent, not guaranteed outcomes. The optional film suffix changes treatment and must not be silently added to a strict reference study.

# Angle Templates Reference

Detailed prompt patterns for each of the 12 camera angles. Use these as templates, adapting subject-specific details from the reference image.

## Template Structure

Each template follows this pattern:
```
Using the provided image, keep the style, environment, lighting, and subject details identical, and modify the camera to match this: [ANGLE DESCRIPTION]
```

Replace bracketed placeholders with image-specific details.

---

## 1. Wide/High Angle

**Purpose**: Establish scale and environment. Subject appears small but commanding.

**Key Elements**:
- Elevated camera position
- Subject geometrically placed using environmental lines
- Background elements as blurred silhouettes
- Emphasize desolate/expansive environment

**Template**:
```
wide cinematic shot from elevated position, [SUBJECT] standing small yet commanding on [ENVIRONMENT FEATURE], [BACKGROUND ELEMENT] as distinct blurred silhouette in distant background, subject geometrically placed using [ENVIRONMENTAL LINES] to draw the eye, emphasizing the vast expansive environment
```

**Example**:
```
wide cinematic shot from elevated position, the warrior standing small yet commanding on vast undulating dunes, spacecraft as distinct blurred silhouette in distant background, subject geometrically placed using the lines of the dunes to draw the eye, emphasizing the desolate expansive environment
```

---

## 2. Full Shot/Bird's Eye

**Purpose**: Show complete subject shape from directly above. Reveal patterns in fabric, shadow, or pose.

**Key Elements**:
- Overhead shot, directly above subject
- Full body visible
- Trailing fabric/materials forming abstract patterns
- Shadows as compositional elements

**Template**:
```
bird's-eye overhead shot showing the full shape of [SUBJECT] and [TRAILING ELEMENT] forming abstract patterns across [GROUND SURFACE] beside [SHADOW/SECONDARY ELEMENT]
```

**Example**:
```
bird's-eye overhead shot showing the full shape of the subject and trailing fabric forming abstract patterns across the sand beside the spaceship's shadow
```

---

## 3. Mid Shot/Editorial Portraiture

**Purpose**: Editorial fashion feel. Focus on upper body, direct engagement with camera.

**Key Elements**:
- Waist up framing
- Direct gaze into lens
- Elaborate details of face/hair/upper wardrobe prominent
- Deeply blurred background
- Sculptural off-camera flash for dramatic light/shadow

**Template**:
```
editorial mid-shot portrait from the waist up, [SUBJECT] gaze intensely fixed directly into the lens, [FACE/HAIR DETAILS] and [UPPER WARDROBE] are the central focus, background deeply blurred serving as minimalist backdrop, distinct 3D pop achieved through sculptural lighting highlighting contours and creating dramatic light and shadow play
```

**Example**:
```
editorial mid-shot portrait from the waist up, her gaze intensely fixed directly into the lens, elaborate white geisha makeup with stark black lines and intricate hair styled with metallic ornaments, sculpted breastplate of armor prominent, background deeply blurred serving as minimalist backdrop, distinct 3D pop achieved through sculptural off-camera flash highlighting the contours of face and the sheen of armor
```

---

## 4. Low Angle/Worm's Eye

**Purpose**: Make subject appear powerful and dominant. Viewer looks up at towering figure.

**Key Elements**:
- Camera at ground level
- Subject towers over viewer
- Lower garments/legs exaggerated
- Sky as background
- Foreground texture (sand, ground) visible

**Template**:
```
dynamic low-angle shot from worm's eye perspective, [SUBJECT] towering over the viewer, [LOWER GARMENT/LEGS] exaggerated creating imposing silhouette against [SKY DESCRIPTION], [GROUND TEXTURE] filling immediate foreground, powerful dominant presence
```

**Example**:
```
dynamic low-angle shot from worm's eye perspective, the cyber-geisha warrior towering over the viewer, sweeping fabric of lower dress and armored leg components exaggerated creating imposing silhouette against the pale sky, gritty desert sand filling immediate foreground, powerful dominant presence
```

---

## 5. High Angle/Wide Panoramic

**Purpose**: Emphasize isolation and scale. Subject as small element in vast landscape.

**Key Elements**:
- High camera position
- Wide panoramic view
- Subject as small silhouette
- Atmospheric haze/light
- Large secondary elements (vehicles, structures) in distance

**Template**:
```
wide panoramic view where [SUBJECT] is a small silhouette against [VAST ENVIRONMENT] and [DISTANT ELEMENT] looming in [ATMOSPHERIC CONDITION]
```

**Example**:
```
wide panoramic view where the subject is a small silhouette against vast dunes and the distant spacecraft looming in hazy desert light
```

---

## 6. Extreme Long Shot/Bird's-Eye

**Purpose**: Maximum environmental emphasis. Subject nearly lost in landscape. Creates disorientation.

**Key Elements**:
- Bird's eye view
- Subject as tiny, almost indistinguishable dot
- Faint footprints or trail
- Large shadow of secondary element
- Dutch tilt for disorientation
- 16:9 cinematic framing

**Template**:
```
ELS bird's-eye view of vast empty [ENVIRONMENT], [SUBJECT] is a tiny almost indistinguishable dot walking towards [FRAME POSITION], leaving only faint [TRACES], [SECONDARY ELEMENT] is a larger distorted shadow cast on [SURFACE] positioned at [OPPOSITE POSITION], dramatic Dutch tilt slicing the horizon enhancing disorientation, cinematic 4K
```

**Example**:
```
ELS bird's-eye view of vast empty desert landscape, the subject is a tiny almost indistinguishable dot walking towards the bottom left corner of the 16:9 frame, leaving only faint footprints, the spacecraft is a larger distorted shadow cast on the dune positioned at the top right, dramatic Dutch tilt slicing the horizon enhancing disorientation, cinematic 4K
```

---

## 7. Medium Shot/Extreme Low Angle

**Purpose**: Isolate face/upper body against vast negative space. Creates tension through framing.

**Key Elements**:
- Extreme low angle
- Focus on eyes and upper body detail
- Subject cropped to fill bottom third of frame
- Pushed to one side
- Severely Dutch tilted horizon
- Upper two-thirds is featureless sky (negative space)

**Template**:
```
MS extreme low angle shot focusing tightly on [SUBJECT]'s [FACE/UPPER DETAIL], cropped to fill the bottom third of the 16:9 frame and pushed to the far [SIDE], horizon line severely Dutch tilted, overwhelming upper two-thirds of frame is featureless [SKY/SPACE] creating immense isolating negative space, cinematic 4K
```

**Example**:
```
MS extreme low angle shot focusing tightly on the subject's heavily made-up eyes and the metallic sheen of neck armor, cropped to fill the bottom third of the 16:9 frame and pushed to the far left, horizon line severely Dutch tilted, overwhelming upper two-thirds of frame is featureless pale desert sky creating immense isolating negative space, cinematic 4K
```

---

## 8. Candid/Eye Level

**Purpose**: Reveal authentic, unguarded moment. Documentary feel.

**Key Elements**:
- Eye level camera
- Natural unposed state
- Subtle gesture (adjusting something, gazing away)
- Documentary, authentic feel
- Softly focused background
- Gentle lighting (not dramatic)

**Template**:
```
candid in-between moment capturing [SUBJECT] in natural unposed state, [SUBTLE ACTION OR GAZE], a rare moment of introspection revealing a glimpse behind the [PERSONA], pose feels authentic and documentary, background softly focused, subtle lighting providing gentle illumination without overly dramatic effects
```

**Example**:
```
candid in-between moment capturing the cyber-geisha warrior in natural unposed state, subtly adjusting a metallic hair ornament or gazing reflectively at the vast desert horizon, a rare moment of introspection revealing a glimpse behind the fierce persona, pose feels authentic and documentary, background softly focused, subtle flash-on-daylight approach providing gentle illumination emphasizing authenticity
```

## 9. Close Up/Backlit Silhouette

**Purpose**: Dramatic rim-lit portrait. Translucent materials glow. Golden hour mood.

**Key Elements**:
- Backlit at sunset/golden hour
- Figure contours glowing
- Translucent fabrics lit from behind
- Low desert/warm light

**Template**:
```
backlit silhouette at sunset, [TRANSLUCENT ELEMENT] translucent and [SUBJECT]'s contours glowing against the low [ENVIRONMENT] light
```

**Example**:
```
backlit silhouette at sunset, the veil translucent and the figure's contours glowing against the low desert light
```

---

## 10. Close Up/Product Contouring A (Main Wardrobe)

**Purpose**: Showcase sculptural form of key wardrobe element. Editorial product photography.

**Key Elements**:
- Camera positioned to highlight sculptural form
- Focus on pronounced curves and angular lines
- Material texture revealed through lighting
- Rim lighting creating sharp silhouette
- Volumetric lighting for three-dimensionality
- Golden Spiral composition
- Detail on fasteners, rivets, construction
- Monochromatic palette emphasizing light/shadow

**Template**:
```
Close-Up shot meticulously detailing [WARDROBE ELEMENT], camera positioned to highlight sculptural form focusing on [SPECIFIC CURVE/ANGLE], material sculpted by precise rim lighting creating sharp clean silhouette against softly blurred darker background, volumetric lighting revealing subtle surface variations, [SMALL DETAILS] in crisp focus showcasing construction, composition follows Golden Spiral placing [KEY CONTOUR] off-center guiding the eye along luxurious lines
```

**Example**:
```
Close-Up shot meticulously detailing the black armored top, camera positioned to highlight sculptural form focusing on the pronounced curve of the chest plate and angular shoulder pad, dark matte composite material sculpted by precise rim lighting creating sharp clean silhouette against softly blurred background, volumetric lighting revealing subtle surface variations from smooth almost liquid cups to more rigid textured plating, detailed fasteners and subtle rivets in crisp focus showcasing engineering, composition follows Golden Spiral placing peak of shoulder curve off-center guiding the eye along sophisticated architecture
```

---

## 11. Close Up/Product Contouring B (Hands/Accessories)

**Purpose**: Detail shot of hands and secondary accessories. Texture emphasis.

**Key Elements**:
- Extreme close-up on hands
- Gloves or hand accessories visible
- Fine material textures (lace, metal)
- Background fades to soft blur
- Shallow depth of field

**Template**:
```
extreme close-up on [HAND ELEMENT] clasped before [BODY REFERENCE], revealing fine [TEXTURE A] and [TEXTURE B] textures, [BACKGROUND] fades into soft blur
```

**Example**:
```
extreme close-up on gloved hands clasped before the armour, revealing fine lace and metallic textures, while the desert background fades into soft blur
```

---

## 12. Close Up/Tight Portrait

**Purpose**: Maximum intimacy and intensity. Face fills frame. Eyes dominate.

**Key Elements**:
- Face filling the frame
- Eyes as absolute focal point (piercing, otherworldly)
- Extreme clarity on makeup/skin details
- Ultra-shallow depth-of-field background
- High-contrast lighting sculpting facial planes
- Hint of framing element (veil, hair)

**Template**:
```
intense raw close-up portrait of [SUBJECT]'s face filling the frame, piercing [QUALITY] eyes the focal point conveying profound depth and [EMOTION], intricate details of [MAKEUP/SKIN DESCRIPTION] and [LIP DESCRIPTION] rendered with extreme clarity, hint of [FRAMING ELEMENT] adding to dramatic effect, background is abstract ultra-shallow depth-of-field blur, high-contrast lighting meticulously sculpting the planes of face emphasizing the intensity of gaze
```

**Example**:
```
intense raw close-up portrait of the cyber-geisha warrior's face filling the frame, piercing otherworldly eyes the focal point conveying profound depth and attitude, intricate details of stylized white makeup with stark black lines and the precise shape of dark lips rendered with extreme clarity, hint of black lace veil framing face adding to dramatic effect, background is abstract ultra-shallow depth-of-field blur, high-contrast off-camera flash meticulously sculpting the planes of face emphasizing the starkness of makeup and unwavering intensity of gaze
```

## Quick Reference: Angle-to-Purpose Map

| Angle | Primary Purpose | Key Technique |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|
| Wide/High | Scale, environment | Environmental lines as composition |
| Full/Bird's Eye | Shape, pattern | Overhead, fabric patterns |
| Mid/Editorial | Fashion, engagement | Direct gaze, sculptural light |
| Low/Worm's Eye | Power, dominance | Towering silhouette |
| High/Panoramic | Isolation, vastness | Small figure in expanse |
| ELS/Bird's-Eye | Disorientation | Dutch tilt, tiny figure |
| MS/Extreme Low | Tension, isolation | Negative space dominates |
| Candid/Eye Level | Authenticity | Natural gesture |
| CU/Backlit | Drama, glow | Sunset rim light |
| CU/Product A | Wardrobe detail | Rim light, Golden Spiral |
| CU/Product B | Accessory detail | Texture, shallow DOF |
| CU/Tight Portrait | Intimacy, intensity | Eyes as focal point |
