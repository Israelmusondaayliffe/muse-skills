## Fabric Types

### Natural Fibers
- **Silk charmeuse:** Liquid drape, high sheen on face, matte reverse. Catches light beautifully. Clingy.
- **Silk organza:** Sheer, crisp, structured. Holds shape. Light passes through.
- **Silk crepe:** Matte texture, subtle grain, elegant drape. Less sheen than charmeuse.
- **Linen:** Visible weave, natural wrinkles, breathable texture. Relaxed luxury.
- **Cotton poplin:** Crisp, smooth, slight sheen. Structured shirts.
- **Cotton jersey:** Soft, stretchy, casual drape. T-shirts, casual wear.
- **Raw denim:** Stiff, dark, visible selvedge edge. Structured until broken in.
- **Washed denim:** Soft, lighter color, natural fading patterns. Relaxed.
- **Wool gabardine:** Tight diagonal weave, smooth face, structured. Suiting.
- **Wool flannel:** Soft, slightly napped, warm appearance. Relaxed tailoring.
- **Cashmere:** Ultra-soft, fine texture, subtle sheen. Luxury knitwear.
- **Boiled wool:** Dense, felted, no visible weave. Structured outerwear.
- **Leather (calf):** Smooth, fine grain, soft drape. Luxury bags, shoes.
- **Leather (lambskin):** Very soft, thin, slight sheen. Jackets.
- **Suede:** Napped, matte, velvety texture. Absorbs light.

### Synthetic and Blends
- **Neoprene:** Technical, structured, no drape. Modern, architectural.
- **Technical nylon:** Lightweight, slight sheen, crisp. Activewear, outerwear.
- **Ponte knit:** Thick, structured stretch. Clean lines, no wrinkles.
- **Satin-back crepe:** Matte crepe face, satin reverse. Versatile.
- **Velvet:** Dense pile, light-absorbing, rich. Directional texture.
- **Sequined fabric:** Reflective, catches every light source. Bold, nightlife.

## Fit Vocabulary

| Term | Visual Effect | Character |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| Oversized | Fabric hangs away from body, volume, movement | Relaxed, streetwear, contemporary |
| Relaxed | Slight ease, not tight, not oversized | Comfortable, effortless |
| Regular | Standard fit, follows body without clinging | Classic, conventional |
| Tailored | Cut close to body contours, structured | Professional, refined, sharp |
| Slim | Narrow through body, minimal excess fabric | Modern, sleek |
| Skinny | Very tight, body-revealing | Bold, specific aesthetic |
| Cropped | Shortened in length (hem above natural point) | Contemporary, proportional play |
| Elongated | Extended in length beyond natural point | Dramatic, editorial, avant-garde |
| Deconstructed | Raw edges, exposed seams, intentionally unfinished | Avant-garde, artistic |
| Structured | Holds its own shape, architectural | Formal, editorial, power |
| Draped | Fabric falls in controlled folds, soft sculpture | Elegant, feminine, artistic |
| Body-con | Tight, body-conscious, revealing shape | Bold, evening, statement |

## Silhouette Types

| Silhouette | Description | Styling Context |
|------------|-------------|-----------------|
| A-line | Narrow at top, widens to hem | Dresses, skirts. Classic feminine. |
| Column | Same width top to bottom | Gowns, slip dresses. Elongating. |
| Cocoon | Rounded, wider in middle | Coats, knitwear. Relaxed luxury. |
| Hourglass | Fitted at waist, balanced top and bottom | Classic feminine. Tailored. |
| Boxy | Straight, no waist definition | Streetwear, Japanese fashion, masculine. |
| Asymmetric | One side different from other | Editorial, avant-garde, statement. |
| Empire | High waist just below bust, flows from there | Ethereal, romantic, vintage. |
| Trapeze | Narrow shoulders, widens dramatically | Mod, 60s, dramatic. |

## Accessory Vocabulary

**Jewelry weight:** Delicate (fine chain, small studs), Statement (bold, chunky, visible from distance), Minimal (one signature piece), Layered (multiple pieces, stacking)

**Bag structure:** Structured (holds shape independently), Soft (collapses without contents), Crossbody (worn across body), Clutch (held in hand), Tote (open top, shoulder carry), Micro (decorative size)

**Shoe silhouette:** Pointed toe, round toe, square toe, platform, block heel, stiletto, flat, chunky sole, minimal, sandal, boot (ankle, knee, thigh), mule, sneaker (minimal, chunky, retro)

## Hair Vocabulary

**Texture:** Straight, wavy, curly, coiled, kinky, textured
**State:** Dry and styled, wet, damp, windswept, slept-on, freshly washed
**Style:** Down and loose, half-up, full updo, low bun, high ponytail, braided, slicked back, side-swept, center-parted, messy, structured
**Detail:** Face-framing pieces, flyaways, baby hairs, loose tendrils, pin-curled, finger-waved

## Makeup Vocabulary

**Level:** Bare/no-makeup, natural/skin-first, soft glam, editorial, graphic/artistic, smudged/lived-in, dewy, matte
**Key descriptors:** Flushed cheeks, soft lip, bold lip, smoky eye, clean skin, defined brow, glossy lid, graphic liner, sun-kissed, porcelain, fresh-faced

## Never Write

- "nice outfit" (what garments, what materials, what fit?)
- "fashionable clothes" (specifics or nothing)
- "stylish dress" (what silhouette, what fabric, what color?)
- "elegant look" (what makes it elegant? describe the actual pieces)
- "trendy" (which trend? describe the actual aesthetic)
- "good-looking" (meaningless to an image model)
