100+ direction starters organized by category. Use these to break sameness when diversifying prompts. Each direction is a one-line creative brief that can be applied to any subject.

## CAMERA & COMPOSITION (15)

1. Extreme macro on a single detail (eye, fingertip, fabric weave, surface texture)
2. Tiny figure in vast landscape, environmental scale dominates
3. Overhead flatlay, organized grid arrangement
4. Through-the-glass shot, condensation or reflection adding layer
5. Reflected in a puddle, mirror, or polished surface
6. Silhouette against dramatic backlight
7. Split frame, two contrasting halves sharing one composition
8. Frame-within-frame using doorways, windows, arches, or natural frames
9. Motion blur on everything except the subject (or vice versa)
10. Tilt-shift miniature effect, real scene appearing toylike
11. Fish-eye distortion, center subject with warped periphery
12. Long exposure trails of light or movement around static subject
13. Double or triple exposure, overlapping realities
14. Negative space dominant, subject occupying less than 20% of frame
15. Extreme wide establishing shot, subject barely visible in environment

## LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE (15)

16. Single harsh spotlight, deep black surroundings
17. Dappled light through leaves or blinds creating pattern on subject
18. Neon glow, multiple colored light sources creating mixed shadows
19. Candlelit intimacy, warm micro-pools of light
20. Overcast flat light, muted tones, quiet mood
21. Golden hour rim light, subject edge-lit against shadow
22. Underwater caustics, light filtering through water surface
23. Lightning flash freeze, storm illumination
24. Screen glow, subject lit only by phone/laptop/TV in darkness
25. Stage lighting, dramatic theatrical spots and gels
26. Industrial fluorescent, clinical greenish cast
27. Fire glow, warm flickering light source
28. Dawn or dusk gradient sky as primary light source
29. Light painting, long-exposure colored streaks
30. Fog or haze diffusing all light sources into soft glow

## NARRATIVE & STORY (15)

31. The moment just before something happens (anticipation)
32. The moment just after something happened (aftermath)
33. Caught in the act, subject unaware of camera
34. Subject aware of camera, direct confrontation
35. The first time (first day, first try, first meeting)
36. The last time (final glance, closing door, packing up)
37. Routine disrupted (unexpected element in familiar scene)
38. Hiding or concealing something from viewer
39. Discovering something new (wonder, curiosity, investigation)
40. Teaching or demonstrating (hands showing, attention split)
41. Waiting (patience, boredom, anticipation visible in body)
42. In transit (between places, between states, in motion)
43. At rest after effort (exhaustion, satisfaction, stillness)
44. Preparing (before the event, ritual, getting ready)
45. Celebrating (joy, achievement, release of tension)

## ENVIRONMENT & CONTEXT (15)

46. Abandoned or decaying version of a normally active space
47. Underwater, everything adapted to aquatic physics
48. Extreme altitude, thin air, vast sky, mountain context
49. Dense urban, compressed space, visual noise, signage
50. Pristine laboratory or clean room, clinical perfection
51. Overgrown, nature reclaiming built environment
52. Under construction, scaffolding, raw materials, work in progress
53. At the edge (cliff, rooftop, shoreline, boundary)
54. Underground, cave, tunnel, basement, subway
55. Moving vehicle interior (train, car, plane, boat)
56. Crowded market or bazaar, sensory overload
57. Empty room, single subject, no distractions
58. Backstage or behind-the-scenes, the unseen infrastructure
59. In the rain, relationship between subject and water
60. Night, artificial light sources as primary illumination

## STYLE & TREATMENT (15)

61. High-contrast black and white, no midtones
62. Desaturated color, almost monochrome with hints of one color
63. Oversaturated, Kodachrome-style vivid color
64. Cross-processed, unexpected color shifts
65. Infrared photography, foliage white, sky dark
66. Expired film look, color drift, light leaks, grain
67. Wet plate collodion, 19th century photographic process
68. Tintype or daguerreotype, metallic surface quality
69. Disposable camera aesthetic, flash, red-eye, imperfect
70. Large format, extreme sharpness, shallow plane of focus
71. Lomography, vignette, light leaks, plastic lens distortion
72. Contact sheet, multiple small frames on one sheet
73. Cyanotype, blueprint blue monochrome print process
74. Risograph print, limited colors, halftone dots, slight misregistration
75. Polaroid transfer, soft edges, color bleeding, organic borders

## CONCEPTUAL & SURREAL (15)

76. Impossible scale (giant subject in tiny world or vice versa)
77. Gravity reversed or absent, floating elements
78. Time frozen mid-action, droplets suspended, objects mid-fall
79. Partially dissolved into particles, wind, smoke, or light
80. Growing from or merging with organic environment

88. Subject existing in multiple states simultaneously
89. Levitation, subject floating without visible support
