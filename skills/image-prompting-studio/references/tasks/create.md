# Task: Create (general image prompts)

Scope: image prompts from an idea, scene, subject or object. General creation and open visual exploration.

Read first: `../prompt-contract.md`. When a model is named, consult only its entry in `../model-profiles.md`.

## Workflow

1. Turn the brief into a specific visual moment: subject, action or state, environment, framing, and the light or graphic treatment that makes the idea read.
2. Explain materials through behavior: transmission, reflections, wear, weight, folds, surface contact. Use photographic terms only for photographic treatments; preserve intentional impossible/absurd premises.
3. For exploration, distinguish open (move between conceptual territories), directed (vary the named dimension), and partially directed (nearby possibilities) requests. A coherent shoot can hold several distinct frames without changing its aesthetic. Use the [creative directions library](../libraries/creative-directions.md) when ideas get repetitive, not as a mandatory checklist.
4. Portraits: match texture specificity to viewing distance, preserve supplied features. Products: construction, finish, scale, support, reflected surroundings. Stickers/icons: intentional background treatment; do not promise transparency unless supported. Keep exact copy explicit when present.
5. Finish with the requested framing/ratio and a small number of relevant constraints.
