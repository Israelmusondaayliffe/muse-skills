# Task: Shot planner (coverage blueprints)

Scope: shot lists and coverage plans from a brief or reference — for people, products, groups, environments. Useful on its own; not a compulsory step before ordinary prompts.

Read first: `../prompt-contract.md`. When a model is named, consult only its entry in `../model-profiles.md`.

## Build the plan

1. Use the supplied concept, subject count, setting, style and required shots. Inspect an available reference before making claims about it. Follow explicit counts and constraints (e.g. all close-ups, same pose). When the brief is sufficient, make practical creative decisions rather than running an intake survey.
2. Choose a coherent shoot or deliberately varied exploration. List the few continuity anchors that matter.
3. For each frame specify framing, camera position, action or state, and what the shot reveals. A product or world can have a static state rather than a human action.
4. Use `../recipes/shot_planner/coverage-methods.md` for the people/product/group/environment banks and `shot-list-template.md` when a structured plan helps. The ten-shot source examples are selectable presets, not required counts. A controlled turntable may repeat action; a natural shoot may vary it.

## Deliver and check

Return the shot list, concise continuity notes, and any missing reference detail. Verify required views, useful distinctions between frames, feasible camera positions, and the stated count. Do not pad with filler shots to complete a preset or call a lexical coverage check proof of a good shoot. A plan is complete without prompts or images; add shot prompts only when requested.
