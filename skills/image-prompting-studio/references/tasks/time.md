# Task: Time variation (age, era, season, decay)

Scope: prompts that change a subject, object or world across age, time of day, seasons, weather, eras, decay or renewal while preserving recognizable continuity.

Read first: `../prompt-contract.md`. When a model is named, consult only its entry in `../model-profiles.md`.

## Workflow

1. Produce one transformation, a matched before/after pair, or a progression as requested.
2. Read the source as a character, object or world. Identify what remains recognizable and what the requested change actually affects. Follow the reference and user direction rather than assuming aging, seasonal change or history requires a new pose, wardrobe or setting.
3. Read `../recipes/time_variation/temporal-methods.md` for aging, time, season, weather, era, decay and renewal. For an older photograph reinterpreted today, use the preserved `source-temporal-templates.md` modernization template with its change/keep choices adapted to the brief.
4. Write each prompt with the source description, target state or time, retained anchors, changed physical/environmental cues and coherent lighting. In a matched comparison, retain camera and composition where useful. In a narrative progression, allow purposeful changes of action and setting. Do not add unrelated temporal axes to fill an old preset.
5. Check temporal order, recognizable continuity, and internal consistency of clothing, technology, architecture, weather and light. Source-ground historical claims when accuracy is part of the task; label imagined futures and uncertain reconstructions. A fictional aging image of a real person is not a prediction of their appearance.

Distinguish a sequence of separate images from a single comparison sheet. Review supplied outputs for observed drift; the user remains the final creative judge.
