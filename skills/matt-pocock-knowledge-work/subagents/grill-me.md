Invocation: user-invoked only

Invoke the `grilling` skill. This is the stateless way into the grilling interview: the same relentless interview as `/grill-with-docs`, but it saves nothing locally and builds no `CONTEXT.md`.

Reach for this when you are **not working in a working directory**: sharpening a plan, a design, a piece of writing, anything with no repo under it. If you are in a working directory, use `/grill-with-docs` instead: it runs the same interview and leaves a paper trail, so it is strictly the better one.
