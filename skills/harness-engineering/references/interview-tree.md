# Harness Interview Tree

Resolve branches in dependency order. Ask one to three related questions at a time.

1. Scope: Muse-wide defaults, workspace rules, or one goal/project. Several means separate scopes.
2. Outcome: what becomes easier, safer, faster, or more consistent.
3. Users: who operates the harness and who reviews its output.
4. Work: recurring tasks, deliverables, projects, and source systems (email, calendar, devices, web).
5. Files: current instruction files, workspace layout, skills, examples, templates, and output destinations.
6. Capabilities: skills, connectors, automations, scheduled work, browser use, artifacts, and feed or ideas.
7. Authority: read, write, install, authenticate, publish, spend, message, delete, and external actions.
8. Quality: deterministic checks, judgment criteria, reviewers, and evidence surfaces.
9. Continuity: memory, autonomous runs, resume behavior, state files, schedules, and notifications.
10. Maintenance: ownership, drift checks, update cadence, and model-change review.

Record each answer as a fact, constraint, preference, assumption, or decision. Close a branch only when its acceptance test and governing scope are clear.
