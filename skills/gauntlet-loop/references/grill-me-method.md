# Grill Me Method

Inspect supplied files and the workspace before asking questions. Ask one material question at a time and prefer questions that expose tradeoffs, failure modes, authority boundaries, evidence standards, and irreversible choices.

Resolve:

- goal, desired transformation, audience, and final use;
- primary and supporting deliverables;
- scope, exclusions, constraints, and preferences;
- trusted references, source standards, and evidence requirements;
- factual, analytical, editorial, strategic, creative, technical, operational, and integration expectations;
- privacy, security, external actions, and approval boundaries;
- resource envelope, session strategy, and user checkpoints;
- acceptance conditions, stop conditions, and dangerous unknowns.

Stop grilling when the plan can be written without dangerous ambiguity. Do not ask the user to decide implementation details that the lead can responsibly determine.
