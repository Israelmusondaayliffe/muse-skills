# Knowledge-Work Bars

Knowledge work is not complete because it is polished or long.

## Factual

- Material claims are traceable.
- Primary evidence is preferred when available.
- Weak sources are replaced or limitations are stated.
- Credible disagreements remain visible.

## Analytical

- Conclusions follow from evidence.
- Assumptions and inferences are labeled.
- Counterevidence and plausible alternatives are addressed.
- Uncertainty is proportional to evidence.

## Decision usefulness

- The work answers the approved questions.
- Recommendations connect to evidence and constraints.
- Risks, tradeoffs, and next actions are explicit.

## Editorial

- Structure serves the audience.
- Information is compressed without hiding uncertainty.
- Terminology and claims remain consistent across the full artifact.

## Verification

Use separate factual, analytical, outcome, editorial, integration, and adversarial perspectives as needed. Polished prose never compensates for unsupported claims.
