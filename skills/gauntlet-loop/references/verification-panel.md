# Verification Panel

Select at least three independent perspectives for a mega-project:

- scope and specification;
- evidence and factuality;
- analysis;
- outcome and usefulness;
- editorial communication;
- technical correctness;
- creative or design quality;
- integration;
- evidence audit;
- adversarial challenge.

Every verifier is a new no-inherited-turns agent and receives only the approved plan, compiled program, final artifacts, source register, evidence archive, acceptance matrix, and reproduction commands.

Verifiers do not repair. A failure creates a repair packet for `gauntlet-run`. A new panel checks the repaired artifacts.
