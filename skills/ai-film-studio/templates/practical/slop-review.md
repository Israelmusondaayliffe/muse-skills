# Slop Review Template

Review target:

| Area | Visible defect | Downstream risk | Earliest likely cause | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | Abrupt shadow or incompatible direction | Unstable grade and identity | Location or lighting rule | Hold, fix, or isolate |
| Geometry | Bent or implausible object | Motion breakdown | Source asset or map | Hold, fix, or replace |
| Local logic | Inconsistent weather, reflection, or material | Continuity drift | Asset state or shot direction | Hold, fix, or retest |
| Texture | Waxy, repetitive, or unstable material | Boiling or plastic motion | Source asset or edit stack | Hold, fix, or regenerate |

Inspect at the intended crop. Do not approve an asset merely because the defect might be invisible in a different use.
