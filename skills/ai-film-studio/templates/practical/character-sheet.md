## Reference composition

- Dominant portrait: a clear three-quarter face view with visible eye detail and catchlights.
- Full-body front: neutral posture and readable silhouette.
- Full-body back or alternate view: consistent body, hair, wardrobe, and state.
- Background and light: neutral, controllable, and free of scene-specific grade.

## Descriptor

Write only durable identity information: build, posture, facial anchors, hair, skin or material detail, and state-defining features. Do not add story-specific camera, grade, mood, or action.

## Test plan

Test this asset in the actual conditions it must survive: varied light, changed distance, movement, and a shared frame. Record the specific evidence needed to approve or reject it.
