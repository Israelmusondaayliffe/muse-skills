# Commercial copy checks

The plugin-owned copy gate. Use on all narrative, outreach, and LinkedIn prose, especially when the `writing-quality` skill is not used for final validation.

1. Trace every result, number, customer statement, credential, and comparison to the approved evidence set. Remove an unsupported claim, or label it as a hypothesis when the format permits one.
2. Keep observed market language separate from the founder's interpretation. Do not present an assumption as a customer fact.
3. Name one audience, one offer, and one next action. Do not hide an unresolved offer or positioning decision inside polished copy.
4. Use personalization only when a named source supports it. Never invent a relationship, event, pain point, or account detail.
5. Keep proof within its real boundary. A pilot, demonstration, process, or credential must not become a result claim.
6. Check that the ask matches the commercial stage and that opt-out, reply, invalid-address, and sequence-cap stop rules remain intact for outreach.
7. Remove prohibited claims and record the reason. Stop when a required commercial decision or evidence source is missing.

Copy passes only when every claim is traceable or clearly labeled, the offer and ask are settled, prohibited claims are absent, and no external action has been authorized by drafting alone.
